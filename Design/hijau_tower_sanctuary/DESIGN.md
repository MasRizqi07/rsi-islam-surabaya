## Brand & Style

This design system translates the physical gravity, clinical precision, and 51-year humanitarian legacy of RS Islam Surabaya A. Yani into an elevated digital interface. Balancing clinical excellence with hospitable Islamic heritage, the visual philosophy moves away from the cold, sterile tropes of typical hospital portals in favor of an architectural sanctuary: grounded, serene, and authoritative.

The aesthetic fuses **Architectural Precision** with **Heritage Editorial Dignity**:
- **Monolithic Balance:** Drawing from the physical 13-story "Graha RSI" tower, layouts emphasize steady vertical rhythm, structural poise, and generous, calming negative space that mitigates patient anxiety.
- **Spiritual Subtlety:** Islamic geometric motifs—specifically the 8-pointed star (*Khatam*)—are deployed as atmospheric watermarks and structural framing rather than decorative clutter, establishing authentic institutional roots.
- **Clinical Confidence:** High-contrast typographic hierarchy and deliberate metallic hair-lines articulate institutional dependability, emergency urgency, and empathetic bedside warmth.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the dual identity of RS Islam Surabaya: deep, calming botanical greens representing life, vitality, and architectural permanence; coupled with warm gold accents signifying golden jubilee wisdom and service dignity.

### Surface Hierarchy & Canvas
- **Canvas (`#FBF8F1` - Ivory):** The warm, non-glare foundation for the primary layout. Replaces clinical stark white to lower eye fatigue during critical healthcare interactions.
- **Soft Mint Surface (`#EEF8F6`):** Secondary structural blocks, clinical callouts, and service navigation backdrops.
- **Card Light (`#FFFFFF`):** High-level interactive elements, elevated cards, and patient chart containers.

### Deep Architectural Greens
- **Tower-950 (`#04231F`):** Midnight evergreen, reserved for deep headers, footers, and maximum-contrast structural anchors.
- **Tower-900 (`#063B36`):** Secondary brand container background, nocturnal clinical states, and premium badge surfaces.
- **Tower-800 (`#0A4F48`):** Primary action color (CTA), selected states, and brand iconography.
- **Tower-700 (`#0F6B61`) & Tower-600 (`#13786D`):** Hover states, interactive highlights, and active tab indicators.
- **Tower-100 (`#D6EFEB`):** Soft contextual fills, selected table rows, and secondary button backgrounds.

### Heritage Gold Accents
- **Gold-700 (`#8A6A14`):** Accessible gold text on light backgrounds (ensures 4.5:1 minimum contrast).
- **Gold-500 (`#C9A24A`):** Hairline architectural borders, fine geometric vectors, and iconography accents.
- **Gold-300 (`#E3CB8A`):** Metric values, headline accents, and stats placed over Tower-900 or Tower-950 surfaces.
- **Gold-100 (`#F4EBD0`):** Soft pill badges, VIP tier indicators, and highlighted alert banners.

### Typography & Functional States
- **Ink (`#10201E`):** Primary text color, delivering deep carbon-green legibility with zero pure-black harshness.
- **Ink-Soft (`#4B5F5C`):** Secondary metadata, captions, timestamps, and input labels.
- **Alert-600 (`#C62F2F`) & Alert-50 (`#FDECEC`):** Emergency medical services (IGD 24 Jam), triage urgency indicators, and form error validation.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs classical high-status editorial serif with crisp geometric sans-serif readability.

- **Playfair Display** carries the hospital's academic, institutional, and Islamic medical legacy across section titles, hero narratives, and statistical milestones. It lends warmth and clinical pedigree.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** powers real-time functional clarity: doctor schedules, lab diagnostic panels, triage guidelines, and form fields.
- **Strict Accessibility Rules:** Playfair Display is never used under 24px to safeguard reading speed for distressed patients or elderly users. All UI actions, buttons, meta captions, and numeric readouts use Plus Jakarta Sans.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts are designed around an 8px spatial grid, echoing the vertical rhythm of the 13-story tower.

### Grid Foundations
- **Desktop (>=1200px):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 48px (`3rem`) margins. Max container width capped at 1320px for optimal reading line lengths.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px margins.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins.

### Tower Vertical Rhythm
Sections rely on clear horizontal banded tiers separated by architectural hairlines (1px `rgba(201, 162, 74, 0.3)`). Content blocks are vertically spaced using generous increments (`space-xl`) to promote calm, deliberate scanning during high-stress medical searches.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through architectural layering and subtle, diffused surface tinting rather than standard grey drop shadows.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas):** Ivory (`#FBF8F1`) foundational canvas.
  - **Level 1 (Structural Sections):** Soft Mint Surface (`#EEF8F6`) defining medical clusters, filter zones, and department sidebars.
  - **Level 2 (Cards & Modules):** Pure Card Light (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px border of `rgba(10, 79, 72, 0.08)`.
  - **Level 3 (Interactive / Hover):** Elevated cards gain a diffused ambient shadow tinted with Tower-950: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(4, 35, 31, 0.08)`.
  - **Level 4 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Actions):** Deep sanctuary lift: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(4, 35, 31, 0.18)` framed with a crisp top border of hairline Gold-500.

- **Islamic Khatam Watermark:**
  Background surfaces may incorporate the 8-pointed Islamic star geometric mesh tiled at 4% to 6% opacity in Tower-800 or Gold-500, giving subtle cultural texture without competing with clinical data.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (`1`)** roundedness. Corner radiuses maintain a measured, architectural discipline:

- **Standard Components (Inputs, Small Badges, Buttons):** `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px) corner radius, preserving the precision of modern clinical instruments and structural stonework.
- **Cards, Panels & Module Containers:** `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius (`rounded-lg`), softening the edges while retaining rectilinear alignment.
- **Large Sanctuary Modals & Bottom Sheets:** `0.75rem` (12px) corner radius (`rounded-xl`).
- **Doctor Avatar Badges:** Contained inside an octagonal geometric bezel (Khatam-derived 8-point contour) or clipped softly with an inner 1.5px Gold-500 border ring.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Clinical Action:** Background Tower-800 (`#0A4F48`), text Ivory (`#FBF8F1`), font Plus Jakarta Sans Semibold. Hover shifts to Tower-700 (`#0F6B61`) with a subtle 1px inset border of Gold-500/40.
- **Secondary Heritage Button:** Background Soft Mint (`#EEF8F6`), text Tower-800 (`#0A4F48`), border 1px solid Tower-800/20. Hover background Tower-100 (`#D6EFEB`).
- **Emergency Button (IGD / Hotline):** Alert-600 (`#C62F2F`) solid fill with pure white typography and pulse glow on hover.
- **Architectural Floor Nav Button:** Monochromatic ghost button with 1px Gold-500 hairline border, transitioning to Tower-900 background on active state.

### Cards
- **Doctor Profile Card:** White card base, 1px perimeter border in Tower-800/10. Doctor photo nested in a signature arch-top portrait frame with an adjacent gold pill denoting clinical sub-specialty. Hover elevates card with Tower-950 tinted drop shadow.
- **Department & Clinic Card:** Mint-surface base, accented with a 1px Gold-500 floor divider separating header from operational hours.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Card Light (`#FFFFFF`) interior, 1px border in Ink-Soft/30. Focused state activates a crisp 1.5px Tower-800 border and a 3px soft tint ring (`#D6EFEB`).
- **Labels:** Plus Jakarta Sans Semibold (14px) rendered in Ink (`#10201E`), with optional hints rendered in Ink-Soft (`#4B5F5C`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Poliklinik Chips:** Soft Mint fill with Tower-800 text, 4px border radius.
- **Jadwal Dokter (Available Today):** Gold-100 background with Gold-700 text and 1px Gold-500/40 border.
- **Emergency / Full Quota:** Alert-50 background with Alert-600 text.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checked state uses Tower-800 fill with crisp white geometric tick or inner dot. Unchecked uses Ivory fill with a 1.5px border of Ink-Soft/40.

### Structural Floor Dividers & Architectural Accents
- Horizontal dividers across profiles, headers, and footer tiers feature a 1px solid stroke in Gold-500 (`#C9A24A`) at 30% opacity, grounding sections in the "Hijau Tower" vertical identity.
